Sorting

Sort the crate and the grid the way the table sorts. In the crate, the dial follows the sort: letters for names, years for years. Every screen is live: sort it, drag the dial, scroll the grid. The dashed buttons under each card are for the sheet, not the design.

A. Where the sort lives

One sort for the crate and the grid; the table keeps its headers. Same control in both views, so it reads as one setting.

B. The dial, sorted by year

Each starts sorted by year. Sort by artist or title from the line above the dial and it turns back into letters.

C. The grid, in groups

The grid has no dial, so this is how it shows where you are in the sort.

D. What you can sort by

The table sorts by title, artist, year, label and format. The crate doesn't need all of them.
