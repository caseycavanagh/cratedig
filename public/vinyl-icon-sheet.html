The icon everywhere

The record mark as the site's icon, set up the way caseycavanagh.com does it: tabs, bookmarks, search, the home screen and link previews. Star the ones you like, then copy your picks.

A. How much of the record at 16 pixels

Tabs, bookmarks and search results draw the icon at 16 or 32 pixels. Seven pieces with even gaps turn to gray mush at 16. The blown-up squares are the real pixels.

B. The tile in a tab

caseycavanagh.com puts its mark on a rounded tile that flips with the browser: dark in a light browser, light in a dark one, so it always stands out. Shown with the pick from A as two rings.

C. Home screen

What “Add to Home Screen” and Safari Favorites use: a 180 pixel square. The phone rounds the corners itself. Always dark, like the crate.

D. Link previews

The 1200 × 630 card a shared link shows in Messages, Slack, Bluesky and the rest. Same type as caseycavanagh.com's card, dark like the crate.
