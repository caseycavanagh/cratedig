Records, styled

Getting the records crate ready for its own home at vinyl.caseycavanagh.com. It doesn't have to look like the main site, so these go further. Everything stays sparse: the crate is the page, and the rest is a few quiet things around it. Star one or two from each group; the about and share stickers open when you click them.
