Pull to open

Grab the front record and pull it up out of the crate to open it, alongside the click and tap that open it today. Every sticker is live: drag down to flip through, then try pulling one out. Click or tap an open record to put it back. Star one from each group.

What starts a pull

Drag down already flips forward and drag up already stands the last record back up, so a pull up has to be told apart from that somehow. Each of these follows the finger the F1 way.

How it follows

What the record does under the finger, and what decides that letting go opens it rather than dropping it back. Each of these starts the G1 way, so it's quick to compare.
