Social share

Share sheets for a social crowd: the classic apps, and the picture going along with the link. Shown sharing a record; the crate gets the same sheet with its own picture. Star what you like, then copy your picks.

The sheet

Where the picture sits and how the apps are laid out. A phone gets a sheet from the bottom; a laptop a panel under the share button.

The pictures

What goes out with the link. The link card is what Messages, X, Facebook and the rest draw on their own. A square post and a story are image files, for the apps that take a picture and not a link (Instagram, Snapchat, TikTok): on a phone they go through the phone’s own share sheet, on a laptop they download.

The apps

Which ones, in order. Messages, WhatsApp and the rest open with the link filled in; Instagram, Snapchat and TikTok take the picture.

The marks

How the app icons are drawn.
